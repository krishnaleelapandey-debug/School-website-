About the school

A tradition of learning and service

St. Thomas School in Khalilabad has served its local community since 1988, with a focus on education, values and the development of each learner.

Our story

From a small beginning to an inter-level school

According to the school's published history, St. Thomas School began in 1988 with 20 students and two teaching staff in a rented premises at Shyam Bazar. It later received permanent affiliation in 2003 and commenced inter classes in 2004.

The school now serves students from LKG to Class XII, with the kindergarten and junior classes at Bardahiya and Classes IV to XII at the Nedula section, as described on the official website.
