Academics

Learning for every stage

The school's published course information follows the curriculum prescribed by the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations for ICSE Class X and ISC Class XII.

Learning pathway

A high-level overview based on the school's official course-of-study page. Subject offerings may vary by class and academic year.

Early years

Kindergarten

Early literacy, numeracy, language, drawing, rhymes, conversation and physical education.

LKG–V

Primary school

Language and literature, mathematics, science, environmental studies, social studies, computer studies, art and physical education.

VI–X

Middle and high school

English, Hindi, mathematics, sciences, history and civics, geography, computer applications and other school subjects.
